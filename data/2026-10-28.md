Abstract: Radio-frequency (RF) digital twins (DTs) enable site-specific wireless propagation prediction by explicitly reconstructing the physical environment and modeling electromagnetic interactions. Although physics-based DTs reduce reliance on exhaustive measurements, systematic bias relative to real radio data arises from environmental uncertainty, material inaccuracies, and geometric abstractions.
